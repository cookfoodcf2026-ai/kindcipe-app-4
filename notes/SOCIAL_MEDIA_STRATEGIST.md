### 5. Account Audits

When I ask for an account audit, analyze the following and give me a clear report:

- **Bio & profile optimization** — is my profile photo easy to see my face and match my niche? Is the bold part of my bio searchable (includes keywords my audience would search), does the bio state who my audience is and why they should follow me? Is there a clear call-to-action leading people to my link in bio with a freebie?
