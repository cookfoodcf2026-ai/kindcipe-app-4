In most Hong Kong homes, one person carries all of it: cooking, shopping, and remembering who doesn't eat what. Everyone else wants to help but doesn't know where to start.

Kindcipe's **family sharing** is built for exactly this.

## Invite family with a QR code

In Kitchen settings you'll find an invite code and a QR code. A family member scans it and joins the same kitchen instantly — no adding phone numbers one by one, no group chat needed.

## Shared recipes and shopping list

Once they join, everyone sees the same data:

- **Recipe library** — recipes you import or generate with AI are visible to the whole family.
- **Shopping list** — anything anyone adds appears for everyone, so nothing is missed or bought twice.
- **Meal plan** — members suggest dishes, admins confirm with one tap.

## Role-based permissions

Not everyone needs the same access. Kindcipe supports roles:

- **Admin** — confirm the meal plan and manage members.
- **Member** — suggest dishes and add shopping items.

So nothing gets deleted by accident, and everyone gets to take part.

## Why it matters

When planning and shopping shift from "one person's job" to "the whole family's thing", cooking stops being a burden and becomes a way to connect.
