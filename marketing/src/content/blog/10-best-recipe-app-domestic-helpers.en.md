The more recipes you have, the harder they are to find. Once your library hits dozens or hundreds, scrolling won't cut it.

Kindcipe uses **categories, tags and filters** to narrow things down fast.

## Categories

Recipes are grouped by cuisine / type, for example:

- Home-style dishes
- Soups
- Rice-cooker recipes
- Vegetarian

## Tags

Every recipe carries tags like "under 15 min", "under 30 min", "easy", "low-calorie", "one-pot". Want something quick? Filter by "under 15 min".

## Filters

Filter by:

- **Cook time**
- **Ingredient** (use up what's left in the fridge)
- **Source** (official / creator / your own / AI)

## Cook from your fridge

The most useful one: photograph your fridge, AI recognises the ingredients, and recommends what you can cook tonight. No shopping needed — use what you already have.

## No more searching

With categories, tags and filters in place, "what's for dinner" goes from "I can't find anything" to "which one shall I pick".

Try filtering your way to tonight's dish.
