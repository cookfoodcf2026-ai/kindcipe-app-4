Every day at five o'clock the same loop starts: "what's for dinner?"

It looks trivial, but for many Hong Kong parents it's one of the biggest daily drains on mental energy.

## Why it's so tiring

- **Pairings** — you need vegetables, protein and soup that go together.
- **Time** — how long do you actually have after work?
- **Tastes** — everyone has preferences and dislikes.
- **Ingredients** — what's left in the fridge? Do you need to shop?

Four variables at once is what wears you down.

## How AI shares the load

Kindcipe hands those four variables to AI:

1. You state headcount, time, tastes and restrictions.
2. AI designs three dishes and a soup that pair well and fit your time.
3. It generates a shopping list so you know what to buy.

"Half an hour of thinking" becomes "three minutes, done".

## What you really save

When you stop spending energy on *what* to cook, you have room to actually enjoy cooking and eating. That's the point.

Try letting AI decide tonight's dinner.
