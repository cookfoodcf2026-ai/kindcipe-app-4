Deciding dinner day by day is inefficient. Every meal you start over: rethink, re-shop, re-cook.

**Weekly meal planning** does it all at once.

## Plan a whole week

On Kindcipe's meal-planning calendar you can:

- Lay out breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week.
- Drag recipes in from your library, or generate them with AI.
- See at a glance which days repeat or lack a dish.

## Shop once

Once the plan is set, Kindcipe automatically turns every ingredient into a **shopping list**, grouped by category (meat, vegetables, seafood, dry goods).

The result: you shop once or twice a week instead of running to the supermarket after work daily.

## Time and money saved

- **Time** — no daily deciding and shopping.
- **Money** — one big shop reduces impulse buys and takeaways.
- **Less waste** — you only buy what the plan needs.

## Start your week

Planning doesn't have to be complicated. Plan the next three days tonight and you'll feel the difference.

Start in your browser — no install needed.
