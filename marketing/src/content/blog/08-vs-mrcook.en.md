You spot a dish on an Instagram Reel or YouTube and want to cook it. What happens next?

Screenshot it, copy it into notes, then type out the ingredients yourself — tedious, and often you never actually cook it.

Kindcipe's **AI recipe import** makes it one step.

## How to import

1. Copy an Instagram / YouTube link, or take a screenshot.
2. Paste it into Kindcipe.
3. AI extracts the **ingredients** and **steps** into a standard recipe.

Done. No typing, no copying.

## After importing

- Saved to your **personal recipe library**, easy to find later.
- Drop it straight into the meal plan.
- Add it to the shopping list and shop to it.

## Why import

Hong Kong home cooks get recipes from everywhere: Instagram, YouTube, a friend's WhatsApp forward. Kindcipe gathers those scattered recipes in one place — ready to plan and shop with.

Found something good? Paste it into Kindcipe.
