Shopping has two classic failure modes: **missing something**, and **paying too much**.

Kindcipe's **auto shopping list** tackles both.

## Generated from your plan

Plan a week of meals and Kindcipe automatically turns every ingredient into a shopping list. No more memorising.

## Auto-categorised

Items are grouped by category:

- Meat
- Seafood
- Vegetables
- Tofu / eggs
- Mushrooms
- Dry goods / staples

At the supermarket you walk one aisle per category and miss nothing.

## Shared by the whole family

One shopping list for everyone: anything added appears for the whole family, so nothing is missed or bought twice. Export or share it in one tap — straight to WhatsApp if you like.

## What you save

- **Nothing missed** — the list comes from your plan, not your memory.
- **Nothing duplicated** — the whole family shares one list.
- **Money** — one big shop, fewer impulse buys.

Next time you shop, generate the list in Kindcipe first.
