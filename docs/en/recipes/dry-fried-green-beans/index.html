Dry-fried Green Beans

Blistered and savoury — great with rice or congee.

Ingredients

  • 400 g green beans
  • 80 g minced pork
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 4 dried chillies
  • 1 tbsp light soy

Steps

  1. Trim the beans and dry-fry until blistered; set aside.
  2. Fry the pork and garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add dried chillies.
  4. Return the beans and add soy; toss over high heat.
  5. Mix well and serve.
