Braised Pork Belly

Shanghai-style red-braised pork belly — rich but not greasy, meltingly tender.

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork belly
  • 30 g rock sugar
  • 3 tbsp light soy
  • 1 tbsp dark soy
  • 4 slices ginger
  • 2 star anise
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine

Steps

  1. Cut the pork belly into chunks and blanch.
  2. Caramelise the rock sugar in a pan.
  3. Add the pork and toss to coat; add ginger, star anise and wine.
  4. Add both soys and water to cover; braise gently for 60 minutes.
  5. Reduce the sauce until glossy.
