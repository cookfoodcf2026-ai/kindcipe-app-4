Salt-baked Chicken Wings

Savoury and moreish — an easy snack or side.

Ingredients

  • 10 chicken wings
  • 1 packet salt-baked chicken powder
  • 3 slices ginger
  • 2 spring onions

Steps

  1. Pat the wings dry and marinate in the powder for 30 minutes.
  2. Lay ginger and spring onion underneath.
  3. Steam over high heat for 15 minutes.
  4. Optional: pan-fry briefly until golden.
  5. Serve.
