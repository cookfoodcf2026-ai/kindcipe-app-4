Soy Sauce Fried Noodles

Wok-hei and rich soy — a Hong Kong favourite.

Ingredients

  • 2 nests thin noodles
  • 100 g bean sprouts
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp dark soy
  • 2 tbsp light soy
  • 1 tsp sugar

Steps

  1. Loosen the noodles in water; drain well.
  2. Mix the soy sauce base.
  3. Heat oil and stir-fry the bean sprouts.
  4. Add noodles and sauce; toss over high heat.
  5. Fold in spring onion and serve.
