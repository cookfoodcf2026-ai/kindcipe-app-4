Morning Glory with Fermented Bean Curd

Pungent, savoury and crisp — a Cantonese classic.

Ingredients

  • 400 g morning glory
  • 2 cubes fermented bean curd
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • Pinch of sugar
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Wash and cut the greens.
  2. Mash the bean curd with sugar.
  3. Fry the garlic until fragrant.
  4. Stir-fry the greens over high heat.
  5. Toss with the bean curd sauce and serve.
