Twice-cooked Pork

Spicy and savoury — pork belly with green peppers, a Sichuan classic.

Ingredients

  • 300 g pork belly
  • 2 green peppers
  • 1 tbsp doubanjiang
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp light soy

Steps

  1. Blanch and thinly slice the pork belly.
  2. Pan-fry the slices until lightly golden.
  3. Add doubanjiang and garlic; fry until fragrant.
  4. Add green peppers and stir-fry.
  5. Season with soy and serve.
