Shrimp & Scrambled Eggs

Silky and sweet — ready in 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 150 g prawns
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 spring onion
  • Salt
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Devein the prawns and season lightly with salt.
  2. Beat the eggs with salt.
  3. Stir-fry the prawns until pink; set aside.
  4. Add the egg and prawns; cook until just set.
  5. Finish with spring onion.
