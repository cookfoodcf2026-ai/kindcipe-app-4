Yuxiang Eggplant

Savoury, sweet, sour and spicy — eggplant that soaks up the sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 aubergines
  • 100 g minced pork
  • 1 tbsp doubanjiang
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp vinegar

Steps

  1. Cut the aubergine into batons; soak in salted water and drain.
  2. Pan-fry until soft; set aside.
  3. Fry the pork, doubanjiang and garlic.
  4. Add sugar, vinegar and a splash of water; bring to a boil.
  5. Return the aubergine and toss until the sauce thickens.
