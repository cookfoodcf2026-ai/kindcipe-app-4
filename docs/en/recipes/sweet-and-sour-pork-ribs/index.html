Sweet & Sour Pork Ribs

Tangy, glossy and loved by kids and adults alike.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork ribs
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 tbsp light soy
  • 4 tbsp water
  • 3 slices ginger

Steps

  1. Blanch the ribs.
  2. Pan-fry until lightly golden.
  3. Add sugar, vinegar, soy, water and ginger.
  4. Braise gently for 20 minutes.
  5. Reduce the sauce until it coats the ribs.
