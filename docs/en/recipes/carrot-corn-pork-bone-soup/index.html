Carrot, Corn & Pork Bone Soup

Sweet and nourishing — a Cantonese soup for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork bones
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 corn cobs
  • 2 dried dates
  • 2 slices ginger

Steps

  1. Blanch the pork bones.
  2. Cut the carrots and corn.
  3. Add everything to a pot with water.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low.
  5. Simmer 75 minutes and season with salt.
