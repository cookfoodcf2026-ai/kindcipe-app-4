Tomato & Scrambled Eggs

The classic Hong Kong home dish — sweet, tangy and perfect with rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 spring onion
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Cut the tomatoes; beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Heat oil, scramble the eggs until just set, then set aside.
  3. In the same pan, cook the tomatoes with sugar and a splash of water until soft.
  4. Return the eggs, toss quickly and finish with spring onion.
