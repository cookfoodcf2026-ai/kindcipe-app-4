Steamed Sea Bass

Delicate, fresh and impressive — a Cantonese banquet favourite.

Ingredients

  • 1 sea bass
  • 5 slices ginger
  • 2 spring onions
  • 3 tbsp seasoned soy
  • 2 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Clean and dry the fish; lay ginger and spring onion on top.
  2. Steam over high heat for 8–10 minutes.
  3. Pour off excess liquid.
  4. Drizzle with seasoned soy and add shredded spring onion.
  5. Pour over hot oil to finish.
