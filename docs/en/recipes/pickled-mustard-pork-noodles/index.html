Pickled Mustard & Pork Rice Noodles

Savoury and appetising — a one-bowl noodle soup.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions rice noodles
  • 1 pack pickled mustard
  • 150 g lean pork
  • 800 ml chicken stock
  • 1 spring onion

Steps

  1. Shred the pork and marinate in soy.
  2. Fry the pork and pickled mustard.
  3. Add the stock and bring to a boil.
  4. Cook the noodles until soft.
  5. Finish with spring onion.
