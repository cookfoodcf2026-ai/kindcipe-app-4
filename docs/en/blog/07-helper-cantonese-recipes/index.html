Bilingual recipes + large cook mode for Cantonese cooking

Your helper wants to cook your family’s dishes, but Cantonese recipes are hard to approach: jargon, vague quantities, tiny text.

Kindcipe’s Cook mode redesigns recipes into something that works in a real kitchen.

Large, bilingual

  • Enlarged text, readable from the counter.
  • Chinese and English side by side, so your helper picks what’s easiest.
  • Clear steps to follow one by one.

Cooking-term translation

Fei seui, bau heung, sau jap, gau hin — all have equivalents. No more searching each term online.

Screen stays on + timers

  • The screen won’t sleep while you cook, so you’re not unlocking constantly.
  • Built-in timers for braising, steaming and baking.

The result

Your helper cooks more confidently, you eat with more peace of mind, and there are fewer misunderstandings. That’s why Kindcipe is designed for Hong Kong families.
