Helper mode: fewer misunderstandings, both languages

How much is “a little salt”? What does fei seui (blanching) mean? For a helper who doesn’t read Cantonese, many Hong Kong recipes are a puzzle.

Kindcipe’s Helper mode turns a recipe into a version both sides understand.

Large, bilingual text

One tap switches a recipe between Chinese and English, side by side. The text is enlarged so it’s readable from the counter. No more looking up every character.

Cooking-term translation

Fei seui, bau heung, sau jap — these terms have standard English equivalents. Kindcipe translates them, so you’re never talking past each other.

A Chinese/English shopping list

Export the shopping list as a bilingual list and send it straight to WhatsApp. Your helper shops to the list and won’t buy the wrong thing.

The result

  • Fewer “I thought you meant…” moments
  • Your helper learns your family’s taste faster
  • You don’t have to cook every meal yourself
