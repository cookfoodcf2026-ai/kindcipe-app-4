One-tap 3 dishes + 1 soup with AI

“What’s for dinner?” is a question every Hong Kong family answers daily. When nobody can decide, takeaway wins.

Kindcipe’s AI Chef turns that decision into a single conversation.

How it works

Open AI Chef and answer a few questions:

  1. How many people?
  2. Any kids or elderly?
  3. Quick or slow-cooked?
  4. Anything you don’t eat?

AI instantly designs a 3-dishes-and-1-soup dinner, with ingredients and steps for each dish.

It follows your tastes

It’s not a random list. AI accounts for:

  • Headcount — portions are scaled automatically.
  • Who’s eating — kid-friendly dishes when there are children.
  • Time — ask for under 30 minutes and it won’t suggest a two-hour braise.
  • Restrictions — no spicy, no pork, all respected.

Then cook it

Pick your menu and switch to Cook mode: the screen stays awake, you get step-by-step guidance, and built-in timers keep you on track. From deciding to cooking, one app.

Next time you don’t want to wrestle with “what’s for dinner”, try one-tap AI meals.
