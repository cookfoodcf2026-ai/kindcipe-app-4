Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Kindcipe → Cancel. Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Kindcipe → Cancel.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. All personal data is removed and the action cannot be undone.

AI won't generate a recipe?

Check your network connection or try again later. The free tier has a daily usage limit.

How do I share my shopping list?

On the shopping list screen → one-tap export/share to send it to family or your helper.

Which languages are supported?

Traditional Chinese and English on the free plan; Filipino (Tagalog) and Bahasa Indonesia are Pro. Switchable in Settings → Display language.
