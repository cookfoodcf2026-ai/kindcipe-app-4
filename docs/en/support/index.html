❓ FAQ

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Kindcipe → Cancel.
Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Kindcipe → Cancel.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. All personal data is removed and this cannot be undone.

AI generation isn’t working?

Check your connection and try again. The free plan has a monthly usage limit.

How do I share the shopping list?

Shopping list → Export/Share, then send it to your family or helper.

Which languages are supported?

Traditional Chinese, English, Filipino (Tagalog) and Bahasa Indonesia. Switch in Settings → Display language.
