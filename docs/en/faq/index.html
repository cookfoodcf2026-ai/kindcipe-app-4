FAQ

What is Kindcipe?

Kindcipe is an AI-powered recipe app for Hong Kong families. Save recipes from Instagram/YouTube with AI parsing, auto-plan weekly meals, generate shopping lists, and support domestic helpers with bilingual large-text cooking mode.

How much does it cost?

Free tier includes basic features. Pro is HK$30/month or HK$288/year, unlocking unlimited imports, domestic helper mode, and HKTVmall integration.

Does it support domestic helpers?

Yes. Domestic Helper Mode provides bilingual large-text recipes, cooking terminology translation, and WhatsApp-ready shopping lists.

How is it different from Cookpad?

Cookpad is a global recipe community. Kindcipe is built for Hong Kong families, focusing on AI import, meal planning, shopping, domestic helper support, and HKTVmall integration.
