Frequently asked questions

What is Kindcipe?

Kindcipe is an AI recipe app built for Hong Kong families, with Instagram/YouTube import, meal planning, a shopping list, and a bilingual helper mode.

How much does it cost?

The free tier covers the basics; Pro is HK$30/month or HK$288/year and unlocks unlimited imports, helper mode and more family members.

Does it support domestic helpers?

Yes. Helper mode offers large bilingual recipes, cooking-term translation, and a one-tap Chinese/English shopping list.
